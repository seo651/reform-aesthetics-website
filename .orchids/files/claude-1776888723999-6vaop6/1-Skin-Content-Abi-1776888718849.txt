About Treatment
Filler Dissolving with Safe & Precise Correction
Filler dissolving is a non-surgical treatment designed to correct or reverse unwanted dermal fillers. Whether the concern is overfilling, uneven placement, or migration, this treatment helps restore a more natural appearance.
Using hyaluronidase, a specialised enzyme that safely breaks down hyaluronic acid fillers, the product is gradually dissolved and naturally absorbed by the body. The treatment is quick, precise and minimally invasive.
