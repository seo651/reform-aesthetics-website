About Treatment
Hand Rejuvenation; Restore Volume & Skin Quality
Over time, hands can lose volume and elasticity, making veins, tendons and wrinkles more visible. Hand rejuvenation treatments using dermal fillers or skin boosters help restore lost volume while improving skin hydration and texture.
Dermal fillers replenish volume and soften the appearance of veins and tendons, while skin boosters deeply hydrate and enhance skin quality. Together, these treatments provide a natural, refreshed appearance with minimal downtime.
✓ Restores lost volume in the hands
✓ Reduces visible veins and tendons
✓ Smooths fine lines and wrinkles
✓ Improves skin hydration and elasticity
✓ Minimally invasive with little downtime
