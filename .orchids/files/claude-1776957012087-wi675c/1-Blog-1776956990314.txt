Fake Botox Warning. Why Choosing a Medically‑Led Clinic Matters 


Recent BBC news has brought a tremendous amount of attention to a serious safety issue over the use of toxin. Approximately 40 people in England developed cases of botulism poisoning after receiving injections with the use of unlicensed, counterfeit toxin style products. According to the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) and the Medicines & Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA), these fake products can cause life‑threatening complications, including difficulty swallowing, slurred speech, breathing issues, drooping eyelids, and facial muscle weakness.
These incidents highlight the importance of choosing a reputable, medically-led clinic for any aesthetic treatment. Choosing a reputable medical clinic significantly eliminates the risk of complications associated with unlicensed or improperly administered products.


Anyone performing the injections should be fully qualified and of a medical background. At Reform Aesthetics Clinc in Leicester all treatments are carried out by Dr Priya Patel who has over 15 years of experience in aesthetic medicine. Dr Priya Patel also works for Derma Medical – a renowned training institute in the UK who teach and train other medical professionals on how to carry out such procedures. 


Why choose us? 


At Reform Aesthetics we strive to ensure the highest quality products and safety to all of our patients, we only use products purchased from licensed UK pharmacies and ensure all products meet MHRA standards for quality and safety ensuring patient safety. 


Do be warned of cheap deals. Lower pricing could compromise your safety. One of the warning signs highlighted by the UKHSA is unusually low pricing. At Reform Clinic, our pricing reflects the high-quality, medically performed procedures we provide to our patients.


Safety Tips for Patients 


Before undergoing any cosmetic procedure, ensure that you know who your injector is and check their credentials. Ensure a thorough consultation is offered, which covers going through a medical history and filling out a consent form whereby all risks and complications are discussed by your doctor.

Are you a Teeth Grinder?  
Bruxism is a medical term used for excessive clenching of your masseter muscles and grinding of your teeth. Excessive clenches have lead to muscular pain and hypertrophy in additional to migraines and premature loss of teeth surfaces. By injecting Toxin into the muscle responsible for clenching this treatment can treat Bruxism by relaxing the hypertense masseter muscles. 
Signs that you could be a grinder 
* Morning jaw tension or soreness
* Frequent headaches and tightness in neck and temples. 
* A Clicking or popping noise during jaw opening and closing
* chipped teeth
* Ear Discomfort

What treatments are available in clinic for hair thinning and hair loss?


PRP (Platelet Rich Plasma) treatment uses platelets from your own blood to stimulate natural hair growth. The extracted Platelets contain growth factors that can support follicles, encourage healthier hair, and improve circulation in the treated area which in turn encourages growth of new hair follicles and improves hair density.
A small blood sample is processed in a centrifuge to isolate the platelet-rich plasma, which is then injected onto the scalp. 
The plasma and platelets cells can also be introduced into the scalp using a microneedling technique this combination offers a two-step rejuvenation. The microneedling encourages collagen production and absorption, while PRP delivers concentrated growth factors directly to the follicles. Collectively PRP and microneedling technique can offer more noticeable improvements than merely PRP alone. Best results are seen after 3 sessions of PRP.


Exosomes can also be injected into the scalp using a microneedling device as another option for managing hair loss. Exosomes are tiny, naturally occurring vesicles released by cells. They are known as the messenger packages cells use to communicate with one another. These packages carry important biological signals such as proteins, lipids, and growth-factor-like molecules that help regulate how cells behave. In aesthetics exosomes are being explored for their potential to support skin quality, improve texture, and enhance overall skin vitality by encouraging healthier cellular activity.
We recommend a minimum of 5 sessions for best results.


Vitamin Injections - Biotin & B12 injections. B-vitamins helps to stimulate healthier stronger hair, skin, and nails. Patients can benefit from biotin injections when they want to support hair strength or address thinning as well as improve skin quality. We offer both biotin and B12 injections at Reform and recommend a course of treatments to see a noticeable difference in hair thickness and quality. By injecting this vitamin intramuscularly ( into the upper arm) it ensures a rapid absorption rate of the product in comparison to taking an oral supplement.
